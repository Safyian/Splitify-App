Privacy Policy

💡 Short version: We collect only what's necessary to run the app. We don't sell your data. Ever.

This Privacy Policy describes how Splittify ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the Splittify mobile application ("the App") available on iOS and Android.

By using Splittify, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Splittify:

Data Type What We Collect Why
Account Information Full name, email address, encrypted password To create and manage your account
Expense Data Expense descriptions, amounts, split details, group names To provide the core bill-splitting functionality
Group Data Group names, member lists, balances To manage shared expense groups
Activity Data Actions performed within groups (expenses added, settlements made) To provide an activity feed for group members
Device Information Device type, operating system (collected automatically) To ensure app compatibility and diagnose issues

We do not collect payment card details, bank account information, government IDs, precise location data, or any biometric data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and authenticate your account securely
  • Provide the core functionality of splitting and tracking shared expenses
  • Send transactional emails — account verification and password reset only
  • Display activity history to you and your group members
  • Improve the App's performance and fix bugs
  • Respond to your support requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any form of automated decision-making that affects you legally or significantly.

3. Emails We Send

We only send transactional emails — we do not send marketing emails or newsletters. The emails you may receive from us are:

  • Account verification — sent once when you register to verify your email address
  • Password reset — sent only when you request a password reset

Emails are sent from noreply@mail.splittify.app via Resend, a transactional email service. Resend processes your email address solely for delivery purposes.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We share data only with the following service providers who help us operate the App:

Provider Purpose Privacy Policy
MongoDB Atlas Secure cloud database storage View Policy
Railway Backend server hosting View Policy
Resend Transactional email delivery View Policy

All providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify. We do not share your data with any other third parties unless required by law.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on MongoDB Atlas servers located in the Asia Pacific (Sydney) region. We implement the following security measures:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we never store plain text passwords
  • All API communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Authentication uses JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with expiry
  • Rate limiting on authentication endpoints to prevent brute force attacks
  • Input validation on all data submitted to our servers

While we implement industry-standard security measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your data to the best of our ability.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account through the App, all your personal data including your profile, expenses, and group memberships will be permanently deleted from our systems, subject to the following:

  • You must settle all outstanding balances before deleting your account
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
  • Expense records shared with group members may remain visible to those members

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — update your name via the Profile screen in the App
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated data via the App
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to certain types of data processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@mail.splittify.app. We will respond within 30 days.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know — what personal information we collect and how we use it
  • Right to Delete — request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out — we do not sell personal information, so this does not apply
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights

To exercise your California privacy rights, contact us at support@mail.splittify.app.

9. Children's Privacy

Splittify is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete that information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
